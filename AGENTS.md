# Duavara agent rules

## Licensing and third-party material

- Only add original material or code, dependencies, APIs, fonts, images, audio, and other assets that are freely redistributable and compatible with the Apache License 2.0.
- Before adding third-party material, verify its license and usage terms. Do not add proprietary, source-available-only, unlicensed, or otherwise incompatible material.
- Record the source, license, and required attribution for third-party assets in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`. Preserve required in-app attribution for data sources.
- Keep any new dependency's license compatible with Apache-2.0 distribution.
